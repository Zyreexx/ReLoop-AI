# ReLoop AI

### The Next-Life Engine for Products

> **ReLoop AI determines the highest-value next life for a product — repair, upgrade, refurbishment, reuse, component recovery, or, only when necessary, recycling.**

---

## ♻️ What is ReLoop AI?

Products such as laptops are often replaced when only one or two components become problematic.

A weak battery, damaged keyboard, thermal issues, or an aging component does not necessarily mean the entire product has reached the end of its useful life.

Existing solutions can provide:

- Repair guides
- Product diagnostics
- Parts information
- Resale or refurbishment information
- Product lifecycle data

But they often leave one important question unanswered:

> **What is the best next life for this specific product, in its current condition?**

**ReLoop AI** is a product-lifecycle intelligence system that answers that question.

It combines:

- 📷 Product photos
- 🔍 Device diagnostics
- 📝 User-reported symptoms
- 🧩 Component-level condition
- 📚 Product and repair knowledge

to generate and compare possible circular pathways.
